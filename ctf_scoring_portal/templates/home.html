The CyberVault Breach

At 03:14 UTC, anomaly detection sentries within the CyberVault high-security enclave registered abnormal traffic signatures. An advanced persistent threat actor, identified as ShadowTrace, executed a coordinated multi-vector intrusion against the sovereign infrastructure.

As an elite cyber defense operative, your mission is to trace the adversary's digital footprint across all vectors of compromise. You must advance through the breach timeline step by step: probe perimeter defenses, decipher intercepted communications, dissect rogue binary artifacts, exploit authentication vulnerabilities, comb forensic access logs for stealthy anomalies, and confront the compromised backup host before the adversary solidifies their persistence.

🔒
Sequential Stage Progression

Challenges are locked in sequence. Operators must successfully breach and capture the flag for the active stage before the next stage container reveals itself. Only completed stages and your current active frontier are visible.

⚖️
Strictly Ascending Scoring

Marks strictly increase with each operational stage (Stage 1 through Stage 6) totalling 100 points. Greater complexity delivers proportionally higher point yields for competitive placement.

💡
Tactical Hint Protocol

Hint 1 provides general guidance and is available to all operatives without deduction. Hint 2 grants direct tactical clarity, but triggers a 50% mark deduction from the stage score if unlocked.

🚩
Cryptographic Flag Standard

All recovered flags conform to the strict format CVT{...}. Flags are cryptographically evaluated on submission and stored securely in backend vaults under AES-256 encryption.
